# 🌐 Civic Pulse – AI-Powered Government Scheme Finder

**Civic Pulse** is a Telugu-first civic technology platform that helps citizens discover government schemes, understand eligibility criteria, and access application guidance in simple language.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://civic-pulse-nine-kappa.vercel.app  
📂 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/sapalapallipreethi-bot/civic-pulse-3-languages-updated

## 🚀 Features

- 🌍 **Multilingual Support** – Switch between Telugu, English, and Hindi.
- 🎯 **Personalized Scheme Recommendations** – Find relevant schemes based on age, state, education, occupation, and other profile details.
- 🔎 **Smart Search & Filters** – Discover schemes by keywords and categories.
- 📋 **Eligibility & Benefits** – Understand scheme benefits, eligibility, and departments in simple language.
- 📑 **Document Checklist** – Track required documents with progress.
- 📝 **Application Guide** – Step-by-step guidance with official portal links.
- 🤖 **AI Chat Assistant** – Ask questions about government schemes in Telugu or English (demo mode).
- ❤️ **Saved Schemes** – Bookmark schemes and access them later.

## 💡 Problem Statement

Citizens often struggle to find government schemes and understand eligibility requirements because information is scattered across different websites and presented in complex language. Language barriers make accessing public services even more difficult.

## 💡 Solution

Civic Pulse simplifies access to government information through a multilingual, user-friendly platform with personalized scheme discovery, simplified explanations, document checklists, and application guidance.
